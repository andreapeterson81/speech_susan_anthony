🧩 Task 1: C-E-R Argument Review

Match the 3 parts of an argument to their definition below:

Argument Part Definition
C = Claim The main statement or belief you argue is true.
E = Evidence Facts, laws, and legal proof that support your claim.
R = Reasoning Explanation of how your evidence proves your claim.
💡 Hint: Claim is your belief. Evidence is your facts and proof. Reasoning is explaining how they connect!

Day 2 Lesson

Day 2: Reading Speech Chunks 1 & 2

Read Susan B. Anthony's speech in small, manageable chunks.

Speech Chunk 1 — The Alleged Crime

"Friends and fellow citizens: I stand before you tonight under indictment for the alleged crime of having voted at the last presidential election, without having a lawful right to vote. It shall be my work this evening to prove to you that in thus voting, I not only committed no crime, but, instead, simply exercised my citizen's rights, guaranteed to me and all United States citizens by the National Constitution, beyond the power of any state to deny."

Speech Chunk 2 — "We, The Whole People"

"It was we, the people; not we, the white male citizens; nor yet we, the male citizens; but we, the whole people, who formed the Union. And we formed it, not to give the blessings of liberty, but to secure them; not to the half of ourselves and the half of our posterity, but to the whole people - women as well as men."
Day 3 Lesson

Day 3: Key Vocabulary & Main Claim

Master hard legal vocabulary terms and locate Anthony's central claim.

📚 Task 1: Legal Vocabulary Cards

Oligarchy (noun)

A small group of people holding all political power in a government.

Disenfranchisement

Taking away someone's right or privilege to vote in elections.

Bill of Attainder

A law that imposes punishment on a group without giving them a trial.

Ex Post Facto Law

A law that retroactively makes an action illegal after it was already done.

🖍️ Task 2: Locate the Central Claim

Review the options below to find the sentence that best states Susan B. Anthony's central claim:

"Webster, Worcester, and Bouvier all define a citizen to be a person in the United States, entitled to vote and hold office."

⭐ [CENTRAL CLAIM] "Being persons, then, women are citizens; and no state has a right to make any law, or to enforce any old law, that shall abridge their privileges or immunities."

"Hence, every discrimination against women in the constitutions and laws of the several states is today null and void."

Day 4 Lesson

Day 4: Logic vs. Fallacies Evaluator

Determine whether Anthony's claims use valid legal reasoning or logical hyperbole (exaggeration).

Claim Card 1

"Excluding women from voting creates an 'oligarchy of sex' where men act as rulers over women in every household."

✔ VALID LOGIC: Men held all political control, which fits the legal definition of an oligarchy.
Claim Card 2

"An oligarchy of sex is WORSE than an oligarchy of wealth, education, or race."

⚠️ EXAGGERATION / HYPERBOLE: Claiming it is worse than all other forms of oppression is an emotional hyperbole.
